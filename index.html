    Cash flow schedule

    Interest is fixed: every period is charged the original principal times the annual rate divided by the number of payments a year, and it does not fall as principal is repaid. Principal is repaid in equal parts, amounts are rounded to two decimals, and the final payment absorbs any rounding difference.

      Loans and consolidated cash flows

      Each loan uses fixed interest on its original principal, repaid in equal parts. Its net amount disbursed (principal less upfront fees) is treated as paid out one period before its first payment. The portfolio XIRR discounts all loans’ cash flows on their actual dates using a 365-day year.
